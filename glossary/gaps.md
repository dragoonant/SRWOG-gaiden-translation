# Gaps and conflicts

## 1. Unverifiable against the actual Kingcom patch

No readme, script, or dialogue screenshot from Kingcom's OGs patch was reachable (romhacking.net translation 5817 and review 6525, GameFAQs thread 79184667, and the otakon273 readme mirror all failed: Cloudflare bot checks or dropped connections). Every "OGs" English form in the TSVs is therefore the Akurasu wiki's form, which the OGs Spirit Commands page shows was written with the fan translation in view, but which may not match the patch letter-for-letter. The user should spot-check against the patched ISO. Highest-value things to verify in-game:

- Spirit command names (Valor/Persist/Wall/Bullseye/Attune/Zeal/Vigor/Guts/Rouse/Daunt/Pierce/Charge; twin: Bond/Rally/Earn/Clarity/Disrupt/Blitz/Sync/Mettle/Hope/Dread).
- Whether dialogue keeps honorifics (-san, -kun, -sama, -chan, -senpai) and how ranks are rendered (Lt./Lieutenant, Major, Captain, Colonel). No evidence either way for Kingcom; camd11's OGG samples show English ranks and no honorifics.
- Pilot skill names (Potential vs Prevail; Support Attack vs Offensive Support; In-Fighter vs In-fight; Commander vs Command; Concentrate vs Mental; Hit & Run vs Hit&Away; E. Save/B. Save vs EN Save/Ammo Save; Learner vs Learning).
- Terrain labels: Akurasu OGs menu page says Air/Ground/Water/Space; OG2nd page and camd11 say Air/Land/Sea(Water)/Space; camd11 column abbreviations Ai/La/Se/Sp imply Air/Land/Sea/Space.
- Stat labels: Akurasu OGs Menu Translations pairs 技量=Defense and 防御=Maneuvering (page itself flags this as possibly swapped); OG2nd page has 技量=Skill, 防御=Defence; camd11 uses Mel/Skl/Mob/Rng/Def/Acc.
- 撃墜数 = "Beat" (Akurasu OGs menu page) looks like a direct in-game string; confirm.

## 2. camd11 (OGG) vs Akurasu/Kingcom-inferred names

camd11 says it follows Kingcom, but its own spirit-legend abbreviations (Va, Fl, Gu, Ir, Fo, St, Ac, Aw, Mc, Sn, Se, Lu, Ga, As, Rp, Fs, Ds, So, Co, Sy, Drv) and the "Guts" menu entry point to Iron Wall (鉄壁), Strike (必中), Awaken (覚醒), Guts (根性), and possibly Direct Strike (直撃), Assault/Assail (突撃) — i.e. closer to the OGG wiki page / Atlus-style names than to Akurasu-OGs "Wall/Bullseye/Zeal/Vigor/Pierce/Charge". Unresolved; needs the patched OGG ISO or Kingcom's OGs ISO.

- フォリア・エスト: Akurasu "Foglia Est" vs camd11 in-game "Folia" (also Akurasu Max Upgrade page "Folia Unit").
- コウタ・アズマ: Akurasu OGs "Kota", OGG/Series "Kouta"; camd11 in-game "Kota".
- コンパチブルカイザー nickname "Compatikaiser" (camd11 dialogue).

## 4. Terms with no English form found

- Japanese names for nearly all per-unit attacks (attacks.tsv jp column is blank except combination attacks and equippable weapons). Akurasu Mech DB pages give English only.
- OGG-only units with no attack list anywhere on Akurasu: G-Compatible Kaiser, G-Thundergate, Excellence Lightning, Excellence Eternal, Ialdabaoth / Ialdabaoth (Shinka) (only romaji "Kishin Mōgeki Ken", "Gōha Kishin Ken" from Flow Chart), Valefor, Agares, Eleos, Fighter Roar, Neo Granzon, Wendigo, Flatfish, Tadpole, Ijin, Gremor, Sitri, Bofry, Halphas, Flaus, Andras, Peirines, Maldict, Glasyalabolas, Extim, Raha Extim, Hypocrisis, Teugatel, Duminas Proton/Triton/Deuteron, Jetzt Einst line, Dark Brain, Swordian's Guard/Dagger, Frons, Tergum, Bartoll.

- Map terrain names other than 荒野 "Wastes" (city, forest, mountain, sea, ruins, space debris etc.) — not on any Akurasu OGs/OGG page.
- Faction/location Japanese forms not in the JP lists fetched: Aggressors, EOTI, Isurugi Industries, Mao Industries, Tesla Reich Institute, Steel Dragons, Crywolves, Beowulves, Project TD, all location names (Izu Base, Aidoneus Island, Elpis, Moon Cradle, White Star, Hellgate…). jp left blank.

- ゲシュタルト (JP OGG unit-list heading for Bartoll/Mironga), 修羅界, 浅草, バルトール事件, 修羅の乱 — no English attested in the sources.
- Rank titles (少尉/中尉/大尉/少佐/中佐/大佐/曹長 etc.) and the honorific policy — nothing attested for either PS2 patch.
- Chapter/scenario titles for OGs1 21–41+ and all of OGs2/2.5 — Akurasu Timetable only lists 1–20 with titles; OGG Flow Chart has all 35 + Final.
- Pilot ranks and callsigns (Assault 1/2, Treue, Bronzo/Aurum/Argent class numbers) — partial; Story page gives Latune 11, Aurum 1, Bronzo 27/28, W15/W16/W17.
- OGs1 Katina/Russel/Giada/Garnet/Latune F-28 Messer colors, Hagane/Hiryu bridge crew names beyond those listed, and generic enemy pilot names (DC Soldier, UCC Soldier, Inspector, etc.) — generic-soldier English forms are by convention only.
